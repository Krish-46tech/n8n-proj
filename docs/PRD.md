# Project: ai-travel-planner

## Market Research
**Target Users:** Frequent travelers and adventure-seekers

**Market Gaps:** Personalized travel planning based on user preferences and real-time data

## Architecture
- Frontend: React with Redux, due to its ability to manage complex state changes and integrate with various APIs for real-time data fetching
- Backend: Node.js with Express, chosen for its scalability, flexibility, and ease of integration with AI services and databases
- Database: PostgreSQL, selected for its strong support of relational data modeling, ability to handle complex queries, and robust support for transactions
